Beyond what we see and hear

Art speaks in more than one language.

A painting can sound like a melody. A melody can feel like a color. Although painting and music use different tools, both can take us through emotions, memories, and imagination.

01 / SEE

The power of color

Colors, shapes, light, and brushstrokes can suggest joy, tension, calm, or longing before we even understand a painting.

02 / HEAR

The feeling of sound

Rhythm, melody, tempo, and harmony shape the atmosphere of a musical piece and influence how we experience it.

03 / FEEL

One emotional language

When we connect what we see with what we hear, a new interpretation emerges—and every person may experience it differently.

Featured inspiration

Vincent van Gogh

What if a painting could have a soundtrack?

Van Gogh's expressive brushstrokes and dramatic colors invite us to imagine movement and sound within a still image. His work offers a starting point for exploring how visual rhythm can remind us of musical rhythm.
