Art in Motion

Where colors become feelings and music becomes visual.

1. Site Name

Art in Motion

I chose the name "Art in Motion" because it represents the connection between painting and music. Although these are different forms of art, both can communicate movement, emotions, and experiences.

The tagline for the website is: "Where colors become feelings and music becomes visual."

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to explore the relationship between painting and music and how both forms of art can communicate emotions, ideas, and experiences without words.

The website will provide information about visual art, music, artists, composers, colors, emotions, and the different elements that connect these two art forms.

The site will also include interactive content that allows visitors to explore connections between paintings, music, and emotions.

3. Scenarios

The target audience includes people who are interested in art, music, creativity, and emotional expression.

  • What painting could represent the feeling of listening to a melancholic piece of music?
  • What kind of music could match the emotions and colors of Van Gogh's paintings?
  • How can color, rhythm, and movement create similar emotions?
  • What musical piece could represent the feeling of a particular painting?

5. Typography

Playfair Display

This font will be used for the main title, section headings, artist names, and important artistic phrases.

Lato

This font will be used for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, and general information because it is easy to read on screens.
